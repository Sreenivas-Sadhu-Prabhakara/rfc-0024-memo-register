RFC 33 · Socket label inspector

Educational subset of the February 1970 naming proposal. A socket combines a 24-bit user, 8-bit host, and 8-bit AEN; even AENs receive and odd AENs send.

Opposite directions are a necessary naming condition, not proof of a connection. Local relabel proposals change only the AEN and must preserve its direction.
