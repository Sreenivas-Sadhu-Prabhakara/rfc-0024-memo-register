The ARC clock notes describe a 24-bit relative counter with 100 µs or 1 ms ticks. The derived wrap periods are 27m57.7216s and 4h39m37.216s.

Two readings reveal a residue modulo 2²⁴. An independently supplied bound on counter advances determines whether one or several counts remain possible.

A unique tick count does not imply exact elapsed time: clock phase, quantization, drift and read uncertainty are outside this model. Readings must come from one counter with no reset or rate change.
